# Anti-Slop Policy
These rules are blocking by default. An exception requires the rule ID, a product-specific rationale, and an approver in product-quality.json.

## card-soup: Card soup

Do not wrap every section or datum in an interchangeable floating card.

## empty-hero: Oversized empty hero

Do not spend the first viewport on oversized type and empty space without product evidence.

## purple-gradient: Default purple gradient

Do not use purple or blue-purple gradients as an unrequested generic visual identity.

## glassmorphism: Decorative glassmorphism

Do not use blurred translucent panels without a product or interaction reason.

## decorative-orbs: Decorative orbs

Do not add gradient spheres, glow blobs, or bokeh as filler decoration.

## fabricated-metrics: Fabricated metrics

Do not invent customers, ratings, growth figures, activity, or testimonials.

## generic-saas-copy: Generic SaaS copy

Do not use interchangeable claims such as revolutionize, supercharge, or all-in-one.

## excessive-rounding: Excessive rounding

Do not apply large pill or rounded-card radii to unrelated controls and sections.

## meaningless-motion: Meaningless motion

Do not animate elements unless motion communicates state, hierarchy, or causality.
